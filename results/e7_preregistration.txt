E7 PRE-REGISTRATION: is the fixed divisor on par with the standard token-count average?
Written 2026-11-23, before any E7 run. Nothing in this file may change after the first E7 result exists;
any later addition goes below a dated line marked "POST-HOC".

PURPOSE
The paper presents the fixed divisor as an alternative to the standard method (global: divide by the
batch's total response tokens), not as a better one. E7 tests the claim "performs on par" as an
equivalence, not as an absence of difference.

WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN (honest disclosure)
The margins below were chosen after seeing E4 (2 seeds, exploratory), where fixed minus global was
-0.0001 and -0.0003 in held-out loss and -0.73 and +1.32 tokens in mean length. E4's seeds (0, 1) are
NOT reused. E7 uses fresh seeds and more generated answers.

SETUP (identical to E4 unless stated)
- Model SmolLM2-135M, Alpaca, same 16,000 training and 1,000 held-out examples (data order seed 0),
  prompt masked, response plus end-of-text token carry the loss.
- 32 answers per update, 2 epochs (1,000 updates), AdamW lr 1e-4 with warm-up and cosine decay, fp16.
- No gradient clipping. (Under clipping the two methods coincide by theorem, as E2 and E4 showed;
  the unclipped case is where they can differ.)
- Methods: global (weight 1/N per token, N = response tokens in the batch) and
  fixed (weight 1/(B mu), B = 32, mu = 57.61, the mean training response length).
- Seeds: 100, 101, 102, 103, 104, 105, 106, 107 (8 seeds). Within a seed both methods see the
  same batch order and the same sampling seed, so each seed gives one paired difference.
- Changed from E4: answers are sampled for all 1,000 held-out prompts (E4 used 500), and every
  answer's length is saved. Sampling: temperature 1, no top-k or top-p, at most 512 new tokens.

PRIMARY ENDPOINTS (both must pass for the claim "on par")
For each seed s:
  D_loss(s) = held-out token-mean loss of fixed minus that of global
  D_len(s)  = 100 x (mean generated length of fixed - that of global) / that of global   [percent]

H1 (loss):   the 90% confidence interval of mean D_loss lies inside [-0.001, +0.001].
             Margin reason: 0.001 nats per token is a 0.1% change in perplexity, about one
             fourteenth of the cost of per-answer averaging seen in E4 (+0.014).
H2 (length): the 90% confidence interval of mean D_len lies inside [-5%, +5%].
             Margin reason: about 3 tokens per answer, and smaller than the shift caused by the
             old gradient-accumulation bug in E4 (micro4, -6.3%).

Test: two one-sided t-tests (TOST) at alpha 0.05 each, on the 8 paired seed differences
(t distribution with n-1 degrees of freedom), which is the same as the 90% interval lying inside
the margin. The claim needs H1 AND H2; requiring both (an intersection-union test) needs no
multiplicity correction.

Outcomes and wording:
- both pass: "the fixed divisor performs on par with the standard method (equivalent within
  +/-0.001 loss and +/-5% length)".
- one passes: state only the passing one as equivalent; the other is reported as "equivalence
  not shown", with its interval.
- neither passes: report the intervals; the paper drops "on par" and says only what the
  intervals show.

INCOMPLETE RUNS
If a run crashes, it is rerun with the same seed (the script resumes). If fewer than 6 complete
seed pairs exist when Kaggle time or quota runs out, the primary tests are reported as
INCOMPLETE together with the intervals from the pairs that exist. No seeds beyond 100-107 are
added to rescue a result.

SECONDARY (descriptive, no pass/fail)
S1. Direction: the theory predicts global is slightly shorter (theory limit -2.9% for global,
    real shifts in E4 were about 1/4 to 1/3 of their limits). Report mean D_len and its sign.
S2. Per-prompt paired length differences, pooled within seed, and median lengths.
S3. Variance cost of the fixed divisor: coefficient of variation of the per-step gradient norm,
    fixed against global (the fixed divisor's gradient size moves with the batch's token count;
    expected higher for fixed).
S4. Sensitivity: H1 and H2 recomputed with E4's seeds 0 and 1 added (10 seeds), labelled
    exploratory.
