Whatever the outcome, every prediction below is reported, including failures.

E5 (learning rate sweep, E1 task, lengths 2 and 8, 32 documents per update)
  Learning rate = base x {1, 1/3, 1/10}, steps {4000, 12000, 40000}; Adam base 3e-3, SGD base 0.5.
  Methods fixed, global, micro4; 5 seeds; paired within seed.

  P1 (gaps do not depend on the learning rate). Theory gaps: global - fixed = +0.0096,
     micro4 - fixed = +0.0891, at every learning rate. Test: for each optimiser and gap,
     paired difference of the gap between scale 1 and scale 1/10. Pass if the 95% interval
     lies within +/-0.002 (equivalence); fail if it excludes zero by more than that.
     (The theory makes this exact for SGD only on quadratic losses; for Adam it relies on
     the slowly varying second-moment assumption.)
  P2 (the common offset is step-size noise). The fixed divisor's measured value minus 0.5
     is smaller at scale 1/10 than at scale 1 (one-sided paired t-test, alpha 0.05), and
     the offset at scale 1/10 is within +/-0.001 of zero. If the offset does not shrink,
     it is not step-size noise and the paper must say its cause is unknown.

E6 (many lengths: 1 to 32, log-normal, mean 6.74, coefficient of variation 0.97)
  Methods micro1, micro4, global, fixed; 32 documents per update; Adam 3e-3, 8000 steps; 5 seeds.
  Theory learned mean length: micro1 2.831, micro4 5.187, global 6.536, fixed 6.736 (truth).
  Theory total-variation distance from the true length law: micro1 0.363, micro4 0.106, global 0.012.

  P3 (mean length). The gap learned mean (method) - learned mean (fixed) matches the theory
     gap (-3.905, -1.549, -0.200) within 10% of the gap, tested on per-seed paired gaps
     (the 95% interval of the measured gap lies inside +/-10% of the theory gap) for micro1
     and micro4; for global, the 95% interval contains -0.200 and excludes 0.
  P4 (whole distribution). For micro1 and micro4 the learned length law is closer to the
     theory's law than to the true law (total variation, every seed).
  P5 (held-out cost). The learned law's KL from the true law, minus the fixed divisor's,
     matches the theory KL (micro1 0.427, micro4 0.037) within 20%.
